Would You Take £25 to Walk Away?

Most of us can price anything in seconds, but ask what it's actually worth and we freeze. A quick test about the mental value of the things in your life, and what it reveals about what you really want.

If I offered you £25 to leave this community right now, no hard feelings, would you take it?

If some part of you paused at that question, good. That pause is the whole point.

We are brilliant at pricing things and terrible at valuing them. £25 is easy: you can picture it, count it, spend it. But what's the thing you get here actually worth to you? A nudge that finally lands. Someone else asking the question you've been avoiding. Realising you're not the only one finding this hard. Try putting a price on any of that.

Here's the honest bit: a community like this is worth exactly what you make of it. It can be a dead corner of your WhatsApp that you scroll past, or one of the most valuable resources you have as a starting coach. Both are the same group. The difference is entirely in what you notice, what you bring, and what you act on.

At the start of this week I challenged you to set yourself some goals and get a little uncomfortable. It's Friday, and my question to you is: did you?

We are all from different places, time zones and cultures, with different habits and different approaches to life. But what brings us together is the same list: learn more, be better, help people, earn more money, and definitely become a better coach.

You might have joined out of curiosity or with a goal, maybe just to connect with others. Either way, something motivated you enough to take action. I want to talk to that part of you and say: don't stop. The journey is not over, and this is a step, not the end point.

So here is what I'd love you to do. Take the next couple of days to reflect on the mental value of the things in your life. What actually moves you forward? What do you keep saying yes to that isn't worth it, and what do you treat as free that's quietly carrying you?

And please, don't take my £25. I certainly wouldn't. Not at this point. The connections and the learning here are worth far more than the note I'd hand you.

My question for you

What's one thing in your life right now that you'd refuse to sell for any reasonable price?

Sit with that. Whatever comes up first, that's where your real priorities live. I'm curious to hear what comes up for you.
