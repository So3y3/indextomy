The thing I hear most

"That's just who I am" might be an old conclusion wearing your name.

A belief repeated long enough stops sounding like a belief. It starts sounding like identity. But familiar doesn't mean true.

When I hear "that's just who I am," I want to know: is it? Is it carved into your bones, or is it a belief you've repeated so often it started to feel like identity?
