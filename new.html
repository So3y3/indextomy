The thing I hear most

"That's just who I am" might be an old conclusion wearing your name.

A belief repeated long enough stops sounding like a belief. It starts sounding like identity. But familiar doesn't mean true.

When I hear "that's just who I am," I want to know: is it? Is it carved into your bones, or is it a belief you've repeated so often it started to feel like identity?

Step 01
N
NOTICE

Okay, hang on. Something just happened there.What did you feel? What changed? Where did the resistance show up? We notice it before we explain it away.

Step 02
C
CHALLENGE

Let's change the angle.What else could this mean? What are you assuming? What happens if we look at it differently? Sometimes one shift in perspective changes everything.

Step 03
M
MOVE

Now let's do something with it.A decision. An experiment. A boundary. A different response. We turn what we've noticed into movement towards what you actually want.
