Got defensive?

Tell me. That's interesting.

Defensiveness. Resistance. Irritation. The sudden urge to explain yourself. The urge to reject a question before it even lands.

Something happened. Genuinely interesting. Not because discomfort means you're wrong, but because reactions carry information.

We can only work with what we notice.

I'm not here to provoke you for the fun of it. I just want us both to see what's actually going on. Once we can see it, we can work with it.

I coach the person who turns up that day.

Some days you need challenge.

Some days you need energy.

Some days you need space.

Some days you need structure.

Some days you need someone to interrupt the loop.

Some days you need me to slow down.

"Hang on. Listen to what you just said."

I generate ideas. I spot patterns. I can usually see several routes through a problem, and I love the unexpected angle nobody's tried yet. If a route isn't working, I'll change it mid-session.

That's the ADHD doing what it does best. Not a badge, not a burden. Just how my brain works, and it turns out to be genuinely useful in a session.

I'm loyal to the client, not the framework.

And I live for the "ohhh shit, that makes sense" moment. We'll get there.

I may respect your goal more than you do right now.

When fear gets loud and comfort gets persuasive, I remember what you told me you wanted. Not because I own it. Because you asked me to.
