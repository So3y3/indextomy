Adam

Kris’s personal AI assistant

Adam is a private AI assistant built by Kris to help manage information, organise everyday tasks and work with authorised services such as Google Calendar and Gmail.

What Adam does

Calendar

Helps review upcoming events, appointments and commitments from an authorised Google Calendar.

Email

Helps identify, summarise and organise information from authorised Gmail accounts.

Organisation

Helps Kris make sense of information and manage everyday tasks and workflows.

Adam connects to services only where explicitly authorised. It is a personal-use assistant, not a public AI service, and access is restricted to authorised users.

Privacy and data

Adam only accesses information from services that have been explicitly authorised by the account owner.

Information accessed through connected services is used to provide Adam’s functionality. It is not sold and it is not used for advertising.

Adam is a private personal-use application and access is restricted to authorised users.
