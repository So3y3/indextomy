Privacy Policy

Adam - Kris’s personal AI assistant

Adam is a private, personal-use AI assistant operated by Kris. This policy explains what information Adam may access through authorised services, how that information is used and the choices available to the authorised user.

1. Who we are

Adam is a private personal AI assistant operated by Kris (Kristian Sokol). Adam is not a public AI service. It exists to help Kris with everyday organisation and information management, and access is restricted to authorised users.

Contact: kristian.sokol@icloud.com · Website: coachkris.co.uk

2. What data Adam may access

When the account owner explicitly authorises it, Adam may access information from connected services, including:

  • Google Calendar data, such as events, dates, times, descriptions and related calendar information.
  • Gmail data, such as email messages, sender and recipient information, subject lines, timestamps and email content where necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Only data required for the requested functionality should be accessed. Adam does not claim or use access beyond the permissions granted through authorisation.

3. How data is used

Information accessed through connected services is used solely to:

  • provide calendar and email assistance;
  • summarise information;
  • identify relevant or important information;
  • help organise tasks and schedules;
  • provide the functionality requested by the authorised user.

Information accessed through Adam is not sold, and it is not used for advertising, profiling or unrelated commercial purposes.

4. Google data

When Adam accesses Google user data through Google OAuth, that access is limited to the permissions granted by the user during the authorisation flow.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing functionality requested by the authorised user. Adam does not access any Google information that has not been granted through OAuth authorisation.

The use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data sharing

Personal information is not sold.

Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Information may be processed by the technical services required to operate Adam and its authorised integrations, such as hosting, processing and the connected services themselves.

6. Data storage and retention

Data may be temporarily processed or stored by the systems required to operate Adam and its integrations.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or as otherwise required by the systems involved.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information, including authenticated access and OAuth-based authorisation where applicable.

No system can guarantee complete security, and this policy does not claim otherwise.

8. User control

The authorised user can revoke Google access at any time through their Google Account permissions: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The authorised user can also stop using Adam or revoke access to connected services at any time.

9. Children

Adam is not intended for use by children. It is a private personal-use application operated for its owner.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The current version is always published on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or how Adam handles information can be sent to kristian.sokol@icloud.com.
