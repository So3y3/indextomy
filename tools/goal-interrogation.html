Goal Interrogation

21 questions your goal has to survive before you commit a minute to it.

Most goals die quietly because they were never interrogated. They were wishes with a deadline. Work through all 21 questions, honestly rather than quickly. If the goal survives, download the sheet and keep it where you will see it. If it does not survive, good: you just saved yourself a wasted quarter.
