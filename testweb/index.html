The Problem

You know more
than you act on.

The issue isn't usually knowledge. It's what happens in the space between intention and action.

You decide something clearly. Then life happens. You get tired, distracted, comfortable, or afraid. And the mind that was so certain earlier starts making a very convincing case for why not today. That negotiation is the pattern. And the pattern is what keeps you stuck, not a lack of willpower or information.

The Loop

Not every thought
deserves to become a decision.

The work starts with noticing the negotiation between what you decided while clear and what your tired, distracted, comfortable or fearful mind argues for later.

"Is there new evidence, or just a new argument?"

The Test

"Decide before you negotiate."

The Rule

"Did I choose it, or did it choose me?"

The Question

"Progress is not only the streak. It is also how quickly you notice and return."

The Redefinition

The Method

Alignment is not
maximum control.

The goal isn't perfect discipline. It's conscious participation in your own life. Sustainable change involves building the internal and external conditions that make a different life easier to live, not harder to maintain. Alignment works across six areas, as one coaching philosophy, not six separate services.

Mind

Notice the self-negotiation. Decide before you negotiate. State before story.

Body

Build a body that gives you more capacity to live your life. Sleep, nutrition, movement, recovery.

Direction

Clarity on what you're actually building toward. Not just what you're moving away from.

Environment

Don't only add better habits. Build better conditions. Your surroundings shape your behaviour.

Growth

I seek discomfort to find comfort. Capability needs evidence. The edge isn't where you think.

Recovery

Return is part of progress. How quickly you notice and come back matters more than never falling.

From restart loops
to a life built on alignment.

I'm Kris. I broke my back at 15. Lost both my parents by 20. Suddenly became guardian to my 13-year-old brother with no warning, no manual.

For a while after that, I was stuck in restart mode. New Day One every other week. Gym, diet, routine, the magic mindset switch. All-or-nothing. None of it stuck long enough to change anything.

Then I figured something out. Instead of starting over again, I just stayed. That's where everything changed.

I went from a pizza chef who barely spoke English to working on $300M superyachts. Made time for things that fulfil me: winning gold in sailboat racing, climbing a mountain barefoot, holding my breath for 3 minutes freediving, landing 16th place worldwide in longevity.

That's what motivated me to get certified. I'm not interested in helping you start over again. I want to help you figure out what keeps happening between knowing and doing, and build a life that doesn't require constant willpower to maintain.
